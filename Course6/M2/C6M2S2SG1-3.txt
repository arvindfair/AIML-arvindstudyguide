However, there are some major problems associated with one-hot encoding. They are as follows:

 

Curse of dimensionality: Performing one-hot encoding on categorical features with a large number of levels leads to the creation of many new columns that are sparse, which, in turn, leads to a high-dimensional data set.
It is always advisable to build a model on the optimum number of features. Otherwise, you will end up building a complex model with poor predictive performance.
 
Dummy variable trap: This situation occurs when two or more variables are highly correlated with one another, and one variable is able to predict the value of the other due to multicollinearity.
This hampers the performance of the model. Therefore, it is necessary to drop one dummy variable to avoid this problem.
