Explainable AI is the field of AI focused on making AI systems' decisions understandable to humans. It aims to build methods that clarify how AI makes decisions and is essential for trust, fairness, and accountability. Explainability can also be referred to as ‘interpretability’ or ‘transparency’. 

 

In this context, there are two types of models - 

White Box Models: They are inherently interpretable because the decision-making process is clear and simple. Examples: Linear regression, logistic regression, decision trees, etc. White-box models usually run faster because they are simpler. They are preferred when trust and transparency is critical (e.g., healthcare). 

 

Black Box Models: These are complex models which provide high accuracy but lack transparency. Examples: Deep neural networks, gradient boosting, LLMs, etc. Black-box AI excels in high-accuracy complex tasks such as image recognition.
