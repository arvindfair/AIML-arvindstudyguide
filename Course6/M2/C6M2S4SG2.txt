The important concepts in PDP can be summarised as given below. 

A partial dependency plot is used to understand how a particular feature influences the predictions of an ML model on average. 

It is a global interpretation method. 

Example: to understand changes in the price with variations in the house size for a model predicting house prices. A PDP shows the average predicted price for different house sizes, keeping all other features constant. It helps answer such questions such as: 

Does a larger house generally lead to a higher price?  

Is the relationship linear or more complex?  

Caveat: PDPs assume that the feature of interest is independent of other features; but what if house size and neighborhood are correlated.
